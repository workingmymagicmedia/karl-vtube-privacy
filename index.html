Privacy Policy — Karl's VTube Software

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Karl's VTube Software is a Windows desktop application developed primarily for private use by its developer for avatar rendering, livestream production, video calls, interviews, and related real-time video applications.

From time to time, access may also be provided to a limited number of invited testers for development and testing purposes. Karl's VTube Software is not currently intended for general public distribution.

Camera Access

Karl's VTube Software accesses a camera or other user-selected video input in order to provide real-time avatar, face-tracking, augmented reality, and visual-effects functionality.

The selected camera input may be provided to Snap Camera Kit so that Lens Studio Lenses can be rendered in real time.

Karl's VTube Software is designed so that the raw camera is not intentionally displayed in Karl's interface or sent directly through its guarded OBS output. The OBS output is intended to contain the Camera Kit Lens-rendered result.

Karl's VTube Software does not intentionally record or permanently store camera footage.

Camera, Microphone, Location, and Media Permissions

The current version of Karl's VTube Software requests camera access when needed for avatar rendering.

The current version does not intentionally request microphone, background location, or media-library access.

If future functionality requires additional device permissions or additional types of information, this Privacy Policy will be reviewed and updated as appropriate before that functionality is generally used.

Data Stored by Karl's VTube Software

Karl's VTube Software does not require a Karl user account and does not maintain a Karl-operated remote database containing user profiles or personal information.

Application preferences may be stored locally on the user's computer. These settings may include:

  • selected camera devices;
  • camera input and output quality settings;
  • Lens identifiers;
  • Snap Camera Kit configuration;
  • Camera Kit agreement acknowledgement;
  • Face Blend settings;
  • avatar and visual adjustment settings;
  • OBS output settings;
  • costume and Lens configuration; and
  • other application preferences.

These settings are stored locally for the purpose of preserving the user's application configuration between sessions.

Snap Camera Kit

Karl's VTube Software integrates Snap Camera Kit, a service provided by Snap Inc., to load and render Lens Studio Lenses and related augmented-reality effects.

Camera Kit communicates with Snap services as part of normal operation. Depending on the Camera Kit functionality provided by Snap, these communications may include Lens delivery, authentication, diagnostics, usage information, and performance information.

Snap's handling of information associated with Camera Kit is governed by Snap's own terms and privacy practices.

Karl's VTube Software does not use Camera Kit information to create advertising profiles, identify individual users for advertising, or track users across unrelated applications or services.

OBS and Virtual Camera Output

Karl's VTube Software may provide its rendered video output to other applications selected by the user, including OBS Studio, virtual-camera software, video-conferencing applications, recording software, and livestreaming software.

Karl provides a local Browser Source for OBS through a localhost address on the same computer. The OBS Browser Source is intended to contain only the Camera Kit Lens-rendered result.

If Karl cannot verify that an appropriate Lens-rendered output is available, the guarded output is designed to fail closed rather than intentionally expose the raw camera.

Once a user chooses to provide Karl's output to another application or service, information handled by that application or service is subject to its own privacy practices.

Local Network Features

Certain Karl's VTube Software integrations communicate with services running locally on the user's computer.

Where these services are configured to operate through localhost or 127.0.0.1, they are intended to remain accessible only from the local computer rather than being intentionally exposed to the public Internet.

Mix It Up Integration

Karl's VTube Software can optionally receive local costume-change commands from Mix It Up through a localhost-only HTTP interface on the same computer.

Karl's VTube Software does not require Mix It Up account credentials and does not receive viewer currency balances, Twitch credentials, or chat history through this integration.

The current integration sends Karl only the requested costume or Lens information needed to perform the local costume change.

Mix It Up is a separate third-party application with its own privacy practices. Users who enable Mix It Up are responsible for their configuration and use of that service.

Information Collection

Karl's VTube Software does not currently:

  • require Karl user accounts;
  • maintain a centralized Karl user database;
  • collect user names, addresses, or profile information;
  • sell personal information;
  • use personal information for advertising;
  • use third-party advertising networks; or
  • intentionally collect analytics about individual Karl users.

The application is presently used by its developer and may occasionally be provided to a limited number of invited testers.

Sharing of Information

Karl's VTube Software does not sell personal information.

Karl does not intentionally publish the user's raw webcam feed to Snapchat or intentionally transmit the raw webcam feed to Mix It Up.

Camera Kit-related information may be processed by Snap as necessary to provide Camera Kit functionality and in accordance with Snap's terms and privacy practices.

Rendered video output may also be provided to third-party applications or services at the user's direction, such as OBS Studio, video-conferencing applications, recording applications, livestreaming platforms, or virtual-camera consumers.

Data Retention

Karl's VTube Software does not intentionally retain recordings of camera input.

Locally stored application settings remain on the user's computer until they are changed, reset, manually removed, or removed as part of uninstalling or deleting the application's local data.

Information handled independently by Snap Camera Kit or other third-party applications is subject to those providers' respective retention practices.

Limited Testing

During development, Karl's VTube Software may be provided privately to a small number of invited testers.

Invited testers are subject to the same privacy practices described in this policy.

Testing does not currently involve Karl creating user profiles, centrally storing tester camera footage, selling tester information, or using tester information for advertising.

Distribution

Karl's VTube Software is currently privately distributed. It is used primarily by its developer and may be provided to a limited number of specifically invited testers for development, compatibility testing, and evaluation.

Karl's VTube Software is not currently offered for general public distribution.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Karl's VTube Software's functionality, distribution, data practices, or third-party integrations materially change.

This policy will be reviewed before the application is made available for broader public distribution or if functionality is introduced that collects, stores, or transmits additional types of user information.

The "Last updated" date above will be revised when changes are published.
